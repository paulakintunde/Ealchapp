<objective>
Promote c2 to a real `Level` across the app's type surface and the database plane, so the level panel's seventh band is a band and not a label (D-32).

Purpose: D-07 requires all seven bands in the panel. `LEVELS` deliberately excludes `'c2'` today, with an authored rationale at `schema.ts:42-46` and two tests that assert the exclusion by name. D-32 reverses that canon: Paul chose the wider option over the narrower display-only one **after the cost was put to him twice**, most recently by a research pass that independently recommended the narrow route. This plan scopes the wider option honestly rather than letting plan 06 discover it.
Output: `LEVELS` with seven members, every derived regex and validator following automatically, the two exclusion tests rewritten to the new decision, and the one DB constraint that blocked c2 authoring dropped in its own migration.

**This does not authorise authoring c2 content.** It makes c2 expressible. The corpus still has zero units past A2, and the panel's C2 row will resolve to zero units and a "bientôt" state exactly as B1, B2 and C1 do (plan 06).
</objective>
