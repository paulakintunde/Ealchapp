### Locked Decisions

**The spine itself**
- **D-01:** Concept C (Trail) is the spine. A band is one column of acts; the unit is a heading you scroll past, not a place you go. Concepts A and B stay on the canvas for the record and are not to be revisited.
- **D-02:** `lessonoverview.tsx` is deleted as a screen. Its content (minutes, difficulty, prerequisites, the blurb, the stats card, the missions count) becomes the pull-up sheet reached from the `Fiche` chip on a trail heading. Board C2.
- **D-03:** The act becomes its own screen, reached in one tap from the trail. It carries the act stepper (previous/current/next), the act title and description, the mission list, and a card naming what leaves for spaced review on completion. Board C3.
- **D-04:** The mission screen keeps the pager exactly as it is — swipe, or Back and Next, with the act ribbon and the reference sheet in the header. This phase does not touch `LessonPager`'s interaction model, only what sits above it.
- **D-05:** Every act row states what it releases into spaced review ("20 mots partis en révision", "6 mots partent à la fin"), so the SRS stops being a silent mechanic. The act screen carries the Den's two numbers, met and mastered, not one.

**The band selector**
- **D-06:** The level panel is the band selector: a chip beside the Den title that opens a full-row panel, one row per band, each with its name, its progress and the paywall named in words rather than drawn as a 9px padlock. Approved on `BS-Sheet` and demonstrated live on `Approved`.
- **D-07:** It carries all seven bands — sons, A1, A2, B1, B2, C1, C2 — and every panel row's touch target must be at or above 44px. This is the decision's whole basis: seven equal columns in 342px give 43.7px, under the minimum, which is why the pill row was rejected outright rather than merely disliked. Verify at OS font scale 1.3, the scale that already clipped `BIENTOT`.

**The trail heading**
- **D-08:** The trail heading follows the **teaching language**. sons, A1 and A2 show the unit's English `title`; B1 and above show the French `sub`. Drive it off `ENGLISH_TAUGHT_BANDS` in `src/content/english.logic.ts` — the same constant that governs the language control — so one constant drives both and a band cannot be English-taught in one place and French-taught in the other.
- **D-09:** The reason this is a decision at all: the C trail heading has one line where `den.tsx` has two, and every unit in every band carries both names. `sons.04` is `"French Consonants"` / `"Les consonnes"`; `a2.07` is `"At the Restaurant"` / `"Au restaurant"`. Dropping to one line forces a choice that the old two-line row never had to make.

**Acte 00, the narrated way in**
- **D-10:** The narrated lesson becomes **acte 00**: a row at the top of a unit's act list, headphone glyph instead of a dot, opening a pre-roll sheet. It had to move — C turns the unit row into a heading carrying one `Fiche` chip, so the `Narré` pill `den.tsx` draws today (`T.narrCta`) has nowhere left to sit. Board C5.
- **D-11:** Acte 00 renders only when the unit has a lesson carrying **both** `narration` and `features` including `'narrated'`. This is not a one-off surface: 41 lessons in the seed already satisfy both conditions, across sons and A1, so acte 00 appears on most of the free curriculum from day one. A unit may declare `narrated` before its script is written — `schema.ts`'s own comment says so, and `den.tsx` already guards on both. A unit whose narration is unwritten shows no acte 00 at all, not a disabled one.
- **D-12:** The pre-roll sheet opens the existing `narrated.tsx` player unchanged. This phase builds the way in, not a new player. The sheet states the real shape of the lesson it is about to play: for `sons.01.l1` that is 7 stages (warm, focus, input, practice, produce, check, cheat), 24 spoken passages and 7 mic moments across 3 stages. The mic never opens on its own — `narrated.tsx` deliberately waits for a tap, and the sheet must not promise otherwise.

**The language control**
- **D-13:** `englishShown(band, pref)` stops being a boolean. Three states: **Français seul** (no translation in the lesson body), **Français et anglais** (the translation prints under each line, the current `true`), and **Anglais à la demande** (the translation exists, hidden until tapped). ## Summary

The phase is less risky than D-29 implies and more risky than the brief implies, in different places.

**Less risky than feared:** the list. The trail's real worst case *today* is A2 at 251 rows (35 unit headings + 216 act rows), not 361 — the seed contains **zero B1 units**; D-29's figure is a projection from `b1.01.l1`'s 12 acts × ~30 planned units. And the list primitive question has a clean answer: `@shopify/flash-list` v2 is a **pure-JavaScript package** (its npm tarball contains no `android/`, `ios/`, `.podspec` or `build.gradle`), it is **new-architecture only** and this project runs `newArchEnabled=true`, and Expo SDK 57 already pins a blessed version (`2.0.2`) in `bundledNativeModules.json`. Because it is not autolinked, it does not move the `runtimeVersion: {policy:"fingerprint"}` hash, so it needs no new dev client and no EAS rebuild — which is exactly the failure class this project has been bitten by before. `getItemType` handles the heterogeneous heading/act rows, `getFirstVisibleIndex()` + `initialScrollIndex` handle scroll restoration by index rather than by pixel arithmetic, and v2 needs no `estimatedItemSize`, which matters because every row height in this app is a function of the OS font scale.

**More risky than stated:** three things nobody named. (1) **D-07's "all seven bands" collides with the type system twice** — `TRACKS` is `['sons','a1','a2']` and `Unit.track` is documented "Absent past a2", so `content.units(track)` cannot source a B1+ trail at all; and `LEVELS` deliberately excludes `'c2'`, with an authored rationale in `schema.ts:42-46` and six validator membership checks hanging off it. (2) **Deleting `app/lessonoverview.tsx` hard-breaks a test at module scope** — `src/content/lesson-siblings.test.ts:39` does `readFileSync('../../app/lessonoverview.tsx')` outside any `test()`, so the file throws ENOENT on import and takes its eight assertions with it, including the only guard on D-27's two-lesson-unit route. (3) **D-17's premise is stale** — the two-state English toggle is already committed (`0b6a1a9`); what is actually uncommitted is the DEV-PREVIEW-B101 overlay and the new `LessonAct.opener` field.

**Primary recommendation:** Add exactly one dependency, `@shopify/flash-list` at the Expo-pinned version, install it with `npx expo install`, and add nothing else. Build the pull-up sheet as an **expo-router route with `presentation: 'formSheet'`** — verified end-to-end through `node_modules` — which costs zero dependencies, gets Android hardware-back for free, and restores the `?key=<lessonId>` deep link that deleting `/lessonoverview` would otherwise take away.

### Which version

Expo SDK 57 pins `"@shopify/flash-list": "2.0.2"` in `node_modules/expo/bundledNativeModules.json:122`. `[VERIFIED]`

Install with **`npx expo install @shopify/flash-list`** — which will resolve 2.0.2, the version Expo validates against SDK 57. Latest is 2.3.2 (published 2026-06-10). Both are JS-only; both have `getItemType` and `getFirstVisibleIndex()`. 2.0.2 lacks `initialScrollIndexParams.viewOffset` (added later), which only costs the ability to land a restored row a few pixels below the top. **Take 2.0.2.** Do not `--force` to 2.3.2 without a reason; `npx expo-doctor` will flag the mismatch.

### The recommendation

Render the trail as **one flat `FlashList` over a heterogeneous row array**, with `getItemType` distinguishing the pools.

| Row type | Contents |
|----------|----------|
| `orientation` | D-19's how-it-works card (dismissible) |
| `placement` | D-19's placement offer (dismissible) |
| `gate` | D-21's pinned gate banner, when the band is locked |
| `heading` | Unit heading + `Fiche` chip + unit seal (D-02, D-08, R-UnitDone) |
| `acte0` | The narrated way in (D-10), when the unit qualifies |
| `act` | An act row (D-05) |
| `boundary` | D-21's end-of-A1 boundary row |

Why `getItemType` matters and not merely "is nice": FlashList recycles view instances **per type**. Without it, a heading view gets recycled into an act row and back, which is exactly the churn that produces the flicker-and-relayout behaviour long lists on Android are blamed for. `[CITED: @shopify/flash-list FlashListProps.ts:231 (2.0.2) / :262 (2.3.2)]`

Why **not** `FlatList` + `getItemLayout`: `getItemLayout` requires you to know every row's offset in pixels, in advance, without measuring. In this app row height is a function of `PixelRatio.getFontScale()` through `typeMetrics()` (`Type.tsx:116-124`), the act title wraps at an unknown length, and heading rows and act rows are different heights by design. There is no honest `getItemLayout` to write. Without it `scrollToIndex` warns and needs `onScrollToIndexFailed` handling (`node_modules/@react-native/virtualized-lists/Lists/VirtualizedList.js:180-190`), which makes the D-29 jump-to affordance a two-pass retry loop. FlashList v2 measures rows itself and needs **no estimate at all**, which is the single biggest reason to prefer it here.

**Fallback if a new dependency is refused:** plain `FlatList` over the same flat array, `keyExtractor` on the row id, `windowSize` tuned down, **non-sticky headings**, and a jump-to built on `scrollToIndex` + `onScrollToIndexFailed`. It will work at 251 rows. It will be measurably worse at 391 and it will need the retry loop.

### Sticky headings on Android specifically

Three separate Android facts, all verified in installed source:

1. **`SectionList` does not stick on Android by default.** `node_modules/react-native/Libraries/Lists/SectionList.js:243-244`:
   ```js
   const stickySectionHeadersEnabled =
     _stickySectionHeadersEnabled ?? Platform.OS === 'ios';
   ```
   It must be passed `true` explicitly. A dev who tests on iOS and ships to Android gets silently different behaviour. `[VERIFIED: installed react-native 0.86.2]`

2. **Sticky headers force `scrollEventThrottle: 1`.** `node_modules/react-native/Libraries/Components/ScrollView/ScrollView.js:1798-1800`:
   ```js
   scrollEventThrottle: hasStickyHeaders ? 1 : this.props.scrollEventThrottle,
   ```
   That is an `onScroll` bridge event **every frame** for the life of the scroll, layered on top of VirtualizedList's own JS-side windowing work. On a 391-row Android list this is the most likely source of scroll jank in the whole phase. `[VERIFIED]`

3. **RN's sticky-header shim carries an Android-specific layout debounce.** `ScrollViewStickyHeader.js:145`: `const debounceTimeout = Platform.OS === 'android' ? 15 : 64;` — evidence that the Android path has needed special handling. `[VERIFIED]`

FlashList sidesteps (1) — its `StickyHeaders` component is its own cross-platform implementation with no iOS/Android default asymmetry — but not (2), since `stickyHeaderIndices` still rides `ScrollViewProps`.

**Recommendation on stickiness:** **do not make the headings sticky in the first wave.** Ship non-sticky, measure on the Pixel 6 at A2's 251 rows, and only then decide. D-29's real requirement is *"the jump-to affordance and the position marker have to be good"* — neither needs stickiness. If a persistent "which unit am I in" indicator is wanted, a **single pinned chip driven by `onViewableItemsChanged`** costs one small view and no per-frame scroll event, versus sticky headers' 60 events/sec. That is the cheaper shape for the same information.

## Q2 — Scroll restoration

### The good news: the common path does not unmount

`[VERIFIED: app/_layout.tsx:114-157, grep for freezeOnBlur/enableFreeze]`

`app/_layout.tsx` uses expo-router's `<Stack>`, which is the react-native-screens native stack. A `router.push()` **keeps the previous screen mounted** beneath the pushed one. Nothing in the app sets `freezeOnBlur`, `enableFreeze()` or `enableScreens()` with custom options — grep across `app/` and `src/` returns zero hits. So:

> trail → `router.push('/act')` → `router.push('/lesson')` → back, back → **scroll position is preserved for free.** No restoration code required for the primary journey.

This should be verified on device before any restoration machinery is built, because if it holds, the machinery is only needed for the edge cases below.

### The break cases, enumerated

`[VERIFIED: grep "'/den'" across app/ and src/]`

| Call site | Why it breaks scroll |
|-----------|----------------------|
| `app/narrated.tsx:223` — `<FocusHeader onClose={() => router.replace('/den')} />` | **`replace` destroys the den entry and mounts a fresh one.** This is acte 00's exit path (D-10/D-12), so it will be hit constantly. |
| `app/narrated.tsx:230` | Same, second exit control |
| `app/lessonoverview.tsx:32` — dead-link fallback | Screen is being deleted; its replacement must not repeat the pattern |
| `app/missions.tsx:33` — dead-link fallback | Same |
| `app/placement.tsx:164` — `router.replace('/den')` | D-19's placement offer returns here |
| `app/home.tsx:443` — `router.push('/den')` | Push, fine — but if the den was popped by a `replace` elsewhere, this is a fresh mount |

Any one of those lands the learner at row 0 of a 251-row trail. That is the bug the restoration exists to prevent, and it is worth stating in the plan which of the five is being fixed by changing `replace`→`back`/`push` versus by restoring position.

### Store the anchor, not the offset

The existing precedent in this repo is `app/speakmap.tsx:251-262`:

```js
const targetY = useMemo(() => {
  const HEADER = 96;                       // hardcoded
  let y = 0;
  for (const [, w] of worlds) {
    const here = w.ixs.indexOf(posIx);
    if (here >= 0) return Math.max(0, y + HEADER + here * ROW_H - 180);  // ROW_H = 118, hardcoded
    y += HEADER + w.stages.length * ROW_H + 26;
  }
  return 0;
}, [worlds, posIx]);
```

applied via `onContentSizeChange` + a once-flag + `scrollTo({animated:false})`.

**Do not copy this for the trail.** `HEADER = 96` and `ROW_H = 118` are pixel constants, and every row's height in this app scales with `PixelRatio.getFontScale()` through `typeMetrics()`. At the OS font scale 1.3 that D-07 requires verifying, the arithmetic is wrong by a growing margin down the list — and it gets away with it today only because `speakmap` has 30 rows, not 391. It is also 30 rows of a *fixed-height* node design, which the trail is not.

**Recommended mechanism** — index-based, font-scale-immune:

- **Capture** on blur with `listRef.current?.getFirstVisibleIndex()` (`FlashListRef.ts:284`, present in both 2.0.2 and 2.3.2). Use the `useFocusEffect` cleanup pattern the codebase already uses at `app/missions.tsx:39`.
- **Store the row's stable id**, not the index — the index shifts if a dismissible card (D-19) disappears between visits. Resolve id → index at restore time.
- **Restore** with `initialScrollIndex` on the FlashList, which lands the list without a visible jump-to-top frame, rather than scrolling after paint.

### Where the position lives

Two existing stores, and the choice follows how long the position must survive:

| Store | File | Persisted? | Fit |
|-------|------|-----------|-----|
| `useUI` | `src/store/useUI.ts` | **No** — plain `create()`, no `persist` middleware | **Recommended for the scroll anchor.** Within-session restoration is the whole requirement; a scroll position that survives a two-week-old relaunch is not information, it is noise. |
| `useStore` | `src/store/useStore.ts` | Yes — `persist`, `name:'ealch-store'`, `partialize` allow-list at 410-440 | **Recommended for the selected band** (which band's trail is open) and for **D-19's two dismissals**. `browseOpen` (line 68-69: *"a UI preference that should survive remounts, not reset to collapsed on every visit"*) and `lessonKeySeen` (line 62-64: *"auto-shows once, ever"*) are the exact precedents for both. |

Also already available and worth not duplicating: `useProgress.resumeByMode` (`useProgress.ts:40`, `ResumeState` at `progress.logic.ts:50-56`) is the persisted per-activity resume pointer, with `route` holding a full URL including params and `at` holding the local day. **D-18's "Reprendre vs start marker" decision reads from here** — `resumeIsFresh()` (`progress.logic.ts:69`) with `RESUME_MAX_AGE_DAYS = 21` is the existing predicate for "does this learner have somewhere to return to". A first-run learner is `resumeByMode.lesson === undefined`, which is the start-marker condition. Do not invent a second first-run flag.

> **Note on `partialize`:** it is an explicit allow-list. A new field added to `AppState` that is not added to `partialize` will silently not persist. That is how D-19's "both dismissals survive a relaunch" gets shipped broken and green.

### The formSheet path, verified end to end

I traced this through installed source rather than trusting docs, because the two sources disagree (see the flag below).

1. **expo-router forwards every sheet prop to native.** `node_modules/expo-router/build/react-navigation/native-stack/views/NativeStackView.native.js:60` destructures `sheetAllowedDetents`, `sheetLargestUndimmedDetentIndex`, `sheetGrabberVisible`, `sheetCornerRadius`, `sheetElevation`, `sheetExpandsWhenScrolledToEdge`, `sheetInitialDetentIndex`, `sheetShouldOverflowTopInset`, `sheetResizeAnimationEnabled`, `unstable_sheetFooter` — and line 213 passes them to the native `Screen` alongside `stackPresentation: presentation === 'card' ? 'push' : presentation`, plus an `onSheetDetentChanged` callback. `[VERIFIED: expo-router 57.0.9]`

3. **Android-specific limits** `[CITED: docs.expo.dev/router/advanced/modals]`:
   - **Maximum 3 detents on Android** (iOS accepts any number).
   - **`sheetGrabberVisible` is iOS-only** — draw your own on Android. `BottomSheet.tsx:77-79` already has one to copy (`40×4`, `borderRadius: 2`, `t.line(18)`).
   - **`fitToContents` requires explicit content sizing** — `flex: 1` is not supported inside a formSheet.
   - **No native stack header and no nested stack navigator inside a formSheet screen** — `headerShown`, `title` and header buttons do not render. Irrelevant here: the app runs `headerShown: false` globally (`_layout.tsx:116`).
   - A known Android issue exists for keyboard-driven resize with `fitToContents` ([software-mansion/react-native-screens#3181](https://github.com/software-mansion/react-native-screens/issues/3181)) — **not applicable**, the Fiche sheet has no text input.

### ⚠ One contradiction, flagged rather than resolved

`node_modules/expo-router/build/react-navigation/native-stack/types.d.ts:609` says formSheet *"will fallback to `modal` on Android"*. The shipped react-native-screens Kotlin says otherwise, and so do the current Expo docs. I read this as a **stale doc comment in expo-router's vendored copy of the navigation types**, not a description of runtime behaviour — but two sources disagree and I have not run it on a device.

**Plan for this explicitly:** make "open a formSheet route on the Pixel 6 and confirm it draws as a dragged bottom sheet, not a full-screen modal" the **first device check in the phase**, before any Fiche content is built. It costs one throwaway route and five minutes, and it is the only load-bearing claim in this document that I could not settle from source alone.

## Q6 — Android traps this project has already hit

### Trap 1 — OS font scale 1.3 silently drops the tail of text

**The guard:** `src/components/type.logic.ts` — the pure half of `Type.tsx`, tested by `src/components/type.logic.test.ts` under `node --test`.

**What it records** (`type.logic.ts:63-85`, measured on a Pixel 9, quoting):

> At OS font scale 1.3, display-role text (cap 1.25) did not grow at all: the glyphs measured pixel-identical to font scale 1.0, while the line box grew from 34.3dp to 43.0dp. Text then no longer fitted the line it had been measured for, and the tail was silently dropped — a flashcard front read « À » for the card « À bientôt ! », and a conjugation prompt rendered « prendre · présent · », losing the pronoun that was the entire question. Eight of nine cards in one deck lost text. […] It is font-scale dependent and NOT monotonic: absent at 1.0, 1.15 and 1.8, present at 1.3.

**The fix already in place:** `typeMetrics()` (`type.logic.ts:87-96`) applies **one** capped scale to glyphs and line box together; `Type.tsx` switches RN's own `allowFontScaling` off so nothing scales twice. Per-role caps are `ROLE` at `type.logic.ts:20-37`.

**What this phase must not do:**
- Never compute a row height, a header height, or a scroll offset from a pixel constant. `speakmap.tsx:254-262` is the in-repo example of exactly that, and it is only safe because it is 30 fixed-height rows. The trail is 251+ variable-height rows. This is the strongest single argument for FlashList's index-based `initialScrollIndex` over a computed `scrollTo({y})`.
- Never use `height:` where the content is text. Use `minHeight:`. See the conflicting guards below.
- Any `numberOfLines` on a trail row title will truncate *more* at 1.3 than at 1.0.

### Trap 2 — `flex` on a `<Text>` clips its tail

**The guard:** `src/content/sons-07-elision.test.ts:735-796`, test `'nothing sits beside the scene bubble French in a row'`.

**What it records** (measured on a Pixel 6 across five markups against the same strings):

```
icon in the row, flexShrink: 1   CLIPS
icon in the row, flex: 1         CLIPS, and collapses "Pardon ?" to "Pa"
icon in the row, row wraps       CLIPS
icon in the row, no flex at all  CLIPS
ICON OUT OF THE ROW              WHOLE, at every length
```

The mechanism: the bubble hugs its content and is capped at `maxWidth: 92%`, so it has no resolved width; Yoga measures the `Text` at its natural single-line width during the hug pass, and the measured text does not re-wrap. The test's own header notes a previous version of it *"required a `flexShrink: 1` wrapper and called it 'the working shape'. It was not working: it was the third of four markups that clip, and it shipped the defect through a2.05 while passing."* It also records that **the clip is non-deterministic** — *"the identical string rendered whole in one position and clipped in another on the same screen"* — so no per-string assertion substitutes for the structural one.

**What the test actually asserts, in two parts:**
1. No `<TX …>` tag in the file carries `flex` or `flexShrink` in an inline `style={{…}}`.
2. No `flexDirection: 'row'` opens in the 400 characters before the literal `>{beat.fr}<` render site. (Anchored on the render site, not on the string — anchoring on the first `{beat.fr}` occurrence, an `accessibilityLabel` hundreds of lines earlier, made an earlier version pass while the bug was reintroduced.)

> **⚠ Scope limit that matters more than the rule: the test reads only `src/components/ScenePlayer.tsx`.** Nothing guards `den.tsx`, `missions.tsx` or `lessonoverview.tsx` today. Every Concept C trail row puts a title beside a number glyph, a chip or a chevron in a `flexDirection:'row'` — the exact shape. **The trail is walking straight into an unguarded instance of a bug this project has already shipped twice.**

**The correct shape, already in the code:** `app/lessonoverview.tsx:160-166` carries the rule as a comment —

```jsx
{/* flex lives on the wrapper, never on a TX, or the title clips */}
<View style={{ flex: 1 }}>
  <TX font="semi" role="label">{…}</TX>
</View>
```

and `den.tsx:207` (`<View style={{flex:1, minWidth:0}}>`) and `missions.tsx:136` do the same. **Note:** that comment lives in a file this phase deletes. Carry the comment forward, or the next author rediscovers it on a device.

**Recommendation for planning:** extend the `sons-07-elision.test.ts` assertion (or add a sibling test built on the same two checks) to cover the new trail row, act row and sheet components. It is a source-text assertion with no RNTL dependency, which is the pattern this repo uses for screens (`lockVisibilityWiring.test.ts:1-4` states this explicitly).

### Trap 3 — the two 44px guards disagree, for good reasons

Both exist; they say opposite things and must not be cross-applied.

| Guard | Rule |
|-------|------|
| `sons-06-muettes.test.ts:472-482` | A 32px header control **must** carry `hitSlop={8}`, because *"32px is the visual size the header needs; without hitSlop the TOUCH target is 32 too, which is under the platform minimum."* |
| `subMission.logic.test.ts:432-437` | The sentence-chip row **must** be `flexGrow:0, flexShrink:0, height: 44` and explicitly **must not** be `minHeight: 44` — *"not a minimum, which let them grow with the row."* |

For **D-07's level-panel rows**, the first rule applies and the second does not: panel rows contain scaling text, so they need `minHeight: 44` (grow with the font scale) — the `height: 44` discipline is for a fixed chip strip. `den.tsx:129` already uses `minHeight: 44` on the current band tabs; that is the shape to carry over.

### Trap 4 — Android sticky-header defaults and per-frame scroll events

Covered in Q1. Restated as a trap because it is a silent iOS/Android divergence: `SectionList.js:244` defaults `stickySectionHeadersEnabled` to `Platform.OS === 'ios'`, and `ScrollView.js:1798` forces `scrollEventThrottle: 1` whenever sticky headers exist.

### Trap 5 — system back on a layered surface

`sons-06-muettes.test.ts:484-491`, test `'system back closes a layered surface instead of leaving the lesson'`. The bug: *"tapping 'See all 16 endings' and pressing back ejected the learner from the lesson rather than returning them to the grid they came from."* The fix in `lesson.tsx:356` is a `BackHandler.addEventListener('hardwareBackPress', …)`.

Directly relevant to D-02's sheet, the level panel (D-06), and the acte 00 pre-roll (D-12) — three new layered surfaces. **A route-based sheet gets this free.** An overlay-based one needs a `BackHandler` per surface, and the existing `BottomSheet.tsx` has none.

Also note `app.json:34`: `"predictiveBackGestureEnabled": false`. Android's predictive-back animation is off, so back is a discrete event, not a gesture with a preview — which is what makes the `BackHandler` approach viable at all, but also means there is no "peek" affordance for free.

## Finding A — D-07's "all seven bands" collides with the type system

Neither of these is mentioned in CONTEXT.md and both are load-bearing for SC-2.

### A1 — `Track` has three members and `Unit.track` is absent past A2

`[VERIFIED: schema.ts:62-63, 1722-1737; content.logic.ts:318-320; dev-preview-b101.json]`

```ts
// schema.ts:60-63
/** Tracks are the three columns of the Beginners' Den. A Level is broader:
 *  corpus items can be tagged b1..c1 long before a track exists for them. */
export const TRACKS = ['sons', 'a1', 'a2'] as const;
export type Track = (typeof TRACKS)[number];
```
```ts
// schema.ts:1725-1729
/** Display grouping for the Den's three beginner columns. Absent past a2. */
track?: Track;
/** The band this unit teaches. … `unitBand(id)` is the reliable read. */
level?: Level;
```
```ts
// content.logic.ts:318-320
export function unitsInTrack(corpus: Corpus, track: Track): Unit[] {
  return corpus.units.filter((u) => u.track === track).sort((a, b) => a.seq - b.seq);
}
```

And the `b1.01` unit in `dev-preview-b101.json` carries **`level: "b1"` and no `track` field at all** (verified by reading the JSON).

**Consequence:** `content.units(band)` — which `den.tsx:42-50` builds its whole data model on — **cannot source a B1+ trail**. It would return `[]` for every band past A2, and the type checker would reject `'b1'` as an argument before it got that far.

**Recommendation:** add a pure `unitsInBand(corpus, band: Level): Unit[]` beside `unitsInTrack` in `src/services/content.logic.ts`, keyed on `unitBand(u.id)` (`schema.ts:384-387`, which the schema itself calls *"the reliable read"*), sorted on `u.seq` (`schema.ts:1730-1734` explains why `seq` and not the id). It is import-free, so it is `node --test`-able with no device — the pattern this repo already uses.

**Do not widen `TRACKS`.** `T.trackLabels` and `T.trackDescs` are typed `{ sons: string; a1: string; a2: string }` (`strings.ts:93-94`) and carry real authored copy at `strings.ts:445-450`; widening the type makes both language tables incomplete and pulls in every other `Track` consumer. Leave `Track` as the Den's legacy three-column concept and let it die with `den.tsx`'s tab row.

### A2 — `LEVELS` deliberately excludes `'c2'`

`[VERIFIED: schema.ts:42-58, 296-308, and six validator sites]`

```ts
// schema.ts:42-47
 * 'c2' is absent on purpose. We author no c2 content: a c2 candidate is not
 * learning French from an app, and pretending the corpus reaches c2 would put an
 * empty band in every level picker. c2 remains meaningful as an exam RESULT,
 * which is why the two lists are split rather than one list doing both jobs.
export const LEVELS = ['sons', 'a1', 'a2', 'b1', 'b2', 'c1'] as const;
```

`LEVELS` is not just a display list. `schema.ts:308` derives `BANDS_RE = LEVELS.join('|')` — *"the band alternation every content id regex is built from"* — and it is the membership test at `schema.ts:386` (`unitBand`), `1888` (playlist `minLevel`), `2073` (theme levels), `2725` (item), `3719` (lesson), `4058` (unit), `4162` (scenario) and `4228` (level ranges). `schema.ts:2652` also maps worlds 1-6 to LEVELS 1:1 for the Speak path.

Separately, a ratified 2026-09-22 curriculum decision states *"c2 becomes a real level: REVERSES the canonical 'C2 = scoring band only' decision. Needs LEVELS + DB enum + tests change before any c2 lesson ships."* That change has **not** been made.

**Recommendation — a finding to raise, not a change to make (the D-24 discipline):** D-07 requires the *panel* to show seven rows. A panel row is display copy plus a progress read plus a route. **It does not require `'c2'` to be a `Level`.** Render the C2 row from the panel's own band list (a display constant local to the panel component, seven entries) and have it resolve to zero units and a "bientôt" state exactly as B1, B2 and C1 will today — which is honest, since the seed has no content past A2 either. Widening `LEVELS` inside this phase would pull in the Postgres enum, the publish validators and every id regex, which is outside the phase boundary and is explicitly someone else's ratified work.

**State this in the plan explicitly** so that "the panel shows C2" is not mistaken for "the corpus supports C2".

### Pattern 1 — Pure row derivation behind a zero-import module

**What:** all row-list construction (which units, which acts, which cards, what order, what each row says) lives in a `src/content/trail.logic.ts` that imports nothing from react-native, zustand or supabase.

**When to use:** always, in this codebase. It is a stated house rule with three existing instances.

**Why it matters here specifically:** it is the only way to test a 391-row trail's composition without a device — and criterion 8 requires a device pass, so device time is the scarce resource. Everything provable on the host should be proven there.

```ts
// Source: the existing rule, src/content/schema.ts:10-11
// "It therefore has ZERO runtime imports. No react-native, no zustand, no
//  supabase, no third-party validator. Exactly the constraint progress.logic.ts
//  [has]"
```

Existing instances to pattern-match: `src/content/type.logic.ts`, `src/content/narration.logic.ts:1-6`, `src/store/progress.logic.ts`, `src/services/content.logic.ts`.

### Pattern 2 — Screen behaviour asserted against source text

**What:** where there is no RNTL infrastructure, screen-level invariants are guarded by reading the `.tsx` file and asserting on its source with a regex.

**When to use:** for the invariants that would otherwise only be caught on a device — "this component is rendered by something", "this control routes rather than going inert", "flex is not on a TX".

```ts
// Source: src/store/lockVisibilityWiring.test.ts:1-12
// "These are JSX behaviours with no RNTL infra to render them (TEST-02 builds
//  that in a later phase), so the guard is the same source-text assertion
//  entitlementDowngrade.test.ts uses for the store write path."
const appDir = resolve(dirname(fileURLToPath(import.meta.url)), '../../app');
const read = (f: string) => readFileSync(resolve(appDir, f), 'utf8');
```

**The trap in this pattern, and it bites this phase:** a top-level `readFileSync` of a file this phase deletes throws at import time and takes the whole test file down (see Q4). **Read the file inside the `test()` body, not at module scope** — `lockVisibilityWiring.test.ts:12` does this correctly with a `read()` helper; `lesson-siblings.test.ts:39` does not.

### Pattern 3 — Two numbers, never one, and never a fabricated bar

**What:** progress is reported as MET (answered right at least once) *and* MASTERED (recognise and produce held for weeks), never blended.

**Why:** `den.tsx:54-59` records the reasoning — *"If the bar were driven by mastery alone it would read 0/N for the first three weeks of daily practice — the exact D1–D14 window where a blank bar makes people quit."* D-05 carries this forward to the act screen explicitly.

The existing derivation to lift verbatim is `den.tsx:60-72` (`itemsPracticed`, `masteredItems(foldCards(attempts))`, `progressOf(unitId)`). Note it builds a `Set` over every lesson's `itemIds` per unit, per render — at 35 units that is fine inside a `ScrollView`; at 391 virtualized rows it must be memoised once for the band, not computed per row.

### Anti-patterns to avoid

- **Pixel arithmetic for scroll position.** `speakmap.tsx:254-262`. Wrong at font scale 1.3 and wrong the moment a dismissible card (D-19) changes the content height. Use `initialScrollIndex`.
- **`flex` / `flexShrink` on a `<TX>`.** See Trap 2. Put it on a wrapping `<View>`.
- **`height:` on anything containing text.** Use `minHeight:`. (`subMission.logic.test.ts:432-437`'s opposite rule applies only to a fixed chip strip.)
- **A new field in `AppState` that is not added to `partialize`.** Persists nothing, silently.
- **A second source of truth for the language preference.** Replace `lessonEnglish`, do not add beside it.
- **Computing `progressOf` inside a virtualized row renderer.** It allocates a `Set` over the unit's items every time a row scrolls into view.
- **Assuming `content.units(band)` works for B1+.** It does not. See Finding A1.
- **Re-deriving the concept.** D-01 closes it.

## Common Pitfalls

### Pitfall 1 — Deleting a screen that a test reads at module scope

**What goes wrong:** `npm test` fails with `ENOENT`, and the failure names a content test file, not the deletion.
**Why:** `src/content/lesson-siblings.test.ts:39` does `readFileSync('../../app/lessonoverview.tsx')` outside any `test()`.
**How to avoid:** retarget the file's source-regex assertions at the replacement surface *in the same commit as the deletion*, and move the `readFileSync` inside the `test()` body (the `lockVisibilityWiring.test.ts:12` shape).
**Warning signs:** `npm test` red with a path that has nothing to do with the change.

### Pitfall 2 — Mapping `lessonEnglish: true` to the wrong new state

**What goes wrong:** every existing B1+ learner (and the dev device) silently changes behaviour on upgrade.
**Why:** three states, two old values, and "on demand" is the intuitively-appealing wrong answer for `true`.
**How to avoid:** `true → 'both'`, `false → 'fr'`. `'onDemand'` has no old value and must be opt-in only.
**Warning signs:** none in code review. Only visible on a device at B1.

### Pitfall 3 — Persisting a new store field without adding it to `partialize`

**What goes wrong:** D-19's dismissals reappear on every relaunch; SC-6 fails on device while every test passes.
**Why:** `useStore`'s `partialize` (lines 410-440) is an explicit allow-list, not a deny-list.
**How to avoid:** every new persisted field gets three edits — `AppState`, `initialData()`, `partialize` — plus a `migrate` step if an old value needs mapping.
**Warning signs:** works in a session, resets after force-stop.

### Pitfall 4 — Computing scroll position in pixels

**What goes wrong:** the trail lands 200px off, worse the further down the list, worse at font scale 1.3.
**Why:** row heights are `typeMetrics()` outputs, which are functions of `PixelRatio.getFontScale()`.
**How to avoid:** `initialScrollIndex` on a row **id** resolved to an index at restore time.
**Warning signs:** correct at scale 1.0 on the dev device, wrong on anyone else's phone. This is the same non-monotonic trap `type.logic.ts:79-81` records.

### Pitfall 5 — Putting the trail title in a row with an icon

**What goes wrong:** act titles lose their last word, non-deterministically, in some positions and not others.
**Why:** Trap 2. Yoga measures the `Text` at natural single-line width during a hug pass and the measurement does not re-wrap.
**How to avoid:** `flex: 1` on a wrapping `<View>`, never on the `<TX>`; keep the trailing glyph out of the text's own row where the design allows.
**Warning signs:** a device screenshot where one row reads whole and an identical string one row down does not.

### Pitfall 6 — Enabling sticky headings and blaming FlashList for the jank

**What goes wrong:** scroll stutters on a mid-range Android device.
**Why:** `ScrollView.js:1798` forces `scrollEventThrottle: 1` whenever sticky headers exist — a bridge event every frame, on top of list windowing.
**How to avoid:** ship non-sticky first; if a "which unit" indicator is wanted, use one pinned chip driven by `onViewableItemsChanged`.
**Warning signs:** jank that scales with scroll velocity rather than with row count.

### Pitfall 7 — A "0 mots" act row

**What goes wrong:** 107 of 423 act rows (25%) read "0 mots partis en révision", and 4 lessons' rows have no tranche at all.
**Why:** `deckTranche` is legitimately empty for acts that teach without releasing corpus items.
**How to avoid:** authored empty-state copy, or omit the line. The house rule (`den.tsx:19-24`) is that a unit reads honestly rather than fabricating a number — the same discipline applies to reading zero where "nothing this act" is the truth.
**Warning signs:** none in code. Visible on the first A2 device walk.

### Pitfall 8 — Treating D-29's 361 as measured

**What goes wrong:** a performance claim is verified against 251 rows and reported as covering 391.
**Why:** the seed has zero B1 units; 361 is a projection.
**How to avoid:** state which row count each claim rests on. Synthesise a 400-row fixture for the perf check if one is wanted.
