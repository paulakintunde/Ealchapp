# Phase 07.1: Concept C Spine Rewrite — Trail, Act Screen & Language Control - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-09-23
**Status:** Ready for planning
**Source:** The approved design canvas, plus decisions ratified in session on 2026-09-22 and 2026-09-23. **Not** gathered via `/gsd-discuss-phase` — the decisions below were already settled on the canvas before this phase existed, and re-asking them would relitigate approved work.

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

Replace the navigation spine. `den.tsx` becomes one trail per level, `lessonoverview.tsx` stops being a screen and becomes a pull-up sheet, and `missions.tsx` becomes an act screen. Carry with it the level panel, acte 00 for narrated units, the three-state language control, the day-one first run, and the paywall's two trail positions.

**This phase does not design anything.** Concept C beat two alternatives (A Ladder, B Spine) on the canvas and was approved, along with 26 artboards and four further decisions. The design source is authoritative: https://claude.ai/artifact/YcQn6EAGDab4rSAqiEe9Wt, boards C1 through C7 plus `BS-Sheet` (the level panel) and `Approved` (the live level-panel demo). Planning should read the canvas and build from it rather than re-deriving a concept.

**What is being replaced, measured.** Today `den.tsx` renders a per-band tab of unit rows, each row carrying a unit title, a French sub, a met/mastered progress block, an optional `Narré` pill and an optional `PREMIÈRE` lock tag. Pressing a row pushes `/lessonoverview?key=<lessonId>`, which pushes `/missions`, which pushes the mission pager. Concept C collapses that to two taps: the trail row opens the act screen, the act row opens the mission.

**Why it is safe to do now, and why that is temporary.** `attempts`, `learning_sessions`, `entitlements` and `subscriptions` are all 0 rows; there are 5 auth users and 1 profile, all test accounts. Every "this would wipe learner progress" constraint is currently protecting nothing, which makes this the cheapest moment there will ever be for a structural rewrite. The constraint itself does not go away — see D-25.

**Scope boundary against Phase 5.** Phase 5 verified passed 12/12 on 2026-09-20 and ratified the gating rule in `05-GATING-RULE.md`. This phase moves where that rule is *shown*. It may not change the rule, `FREE_BANDS`, `PREMIERE_FEATURES`, `drillDeckGate`, `levelLocked`, `roleplayLocked` or `examPaperAllowed`.

</domain>

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### The spine itself

- **D-01:** Concept C (Trail) is the spine. A band is one column of acts; the unit is a heading you scroll past, not a place you go. Concepts A and B stay on the canvas for the record and are not to be revisited.
- **D-02:** `lessonoverview.tsx` is deleted as a screen. Its content (minutes, difficulty, prerequisites, the blurb, the stats card, the missions count) becomes the pull-up sheet reached from the `Fiche` chip on a trail heading. Board C2.
- **D-03:** The act becomes its own screen, reached in one tap from the trail. It carries the act stepper (previous/current/next), the act title and description, the mission list, and a card naming what leaves for spaced review on completion. Board C3.
- **D-04:** The mission screen keeps the pager exactly as it is — swipe, or Back and Next, with the act ribbon and the reference sheet in the header. This phase does not touch `LessonPager`'s interaction model, only what sits above it.
- **D-05:** Every act row states what it releases into spaced review ("20 mots partis en révision", "6 mots partent à la fin"), so the SRS stops being a silent mechanic. The act screen carries the Den's two numbers, met and mastered, not one.

### The band selector

- **D-06:** The level panel is the band selector: a chip beside the Den title that opens a full-row panel, one row per band, each with its name, its progress and the paywall named in words rather than drawn as a 9px padlock. Approved on `BS-Sheet` and demonstrated live on `Approved`.
- **D-07:** It carries all seven bands — sons, A1, A2, B1, B2, C1, C2 — and every panel row's touch target must be at or above 44px. This is the decision's whole basis: seven equal columns in 342px give 43.7px, under the minimum, which is why the pill row was rejected outright rather than merely disliked. Verify at OS font scale 1.3, the scale that already clipped `BIENTOT`.

### The trail heading

- **D-08:** The trail heading follows the **teaching language**. sons, A1 and A2 show the unit's English `title`; B1 and above show the French `sub`. Drive it off `ENGLISH_TAUGHT_BANDS` in `src/content/english.logic.ts` — the same constant that governs the language control — so one constant drives both and a band cannot be English-taught in one place and French-taught in the other.
- **D-09:** The reason this is a decision at all: the C trail heading has one line where `den.tsx` has two, and every unit in every band carries both names. `sons.04` is `"French Consonants"` / `"Les consonnes"`; `a2.07` is `"At the Restaurant"` / `"Au restaurant"`. Dropping to one line forces a choice that the old two-line row never had to make.

### Acte 00, the narrated way in

- **D-10:** The narrated lesson becomes **acte 00**: a row at the top of a unit's act list, headphone glyph instead of a dot, opening a pre-roll sheet. It had to move — C turns the unit row into a heading carrying one `Fiche` chip, so the `Narré` pill `den.tsx` draws today (`T.narrCta`) has nowhere left to sit. Board C5.
- **D-11:** Acte 00 renders only when the unit has a lesson carrying **both** `narration` and `features` including `'narrated'`. This is not a one-off surface: 41 lessons in the seed already satisfy both conditions, across sons and A1, so acte 00 appears on most of the free curriculum from day one. A unit may declare `narrated` before its script is written — `schema.ts`'s own comment says so, and `den.tsx` already guards on both. A unit whose narration is unwritten shows no acte 00 at all, not a disabled one.
- **D-12:** The pre-roll sheet opens the existing `narrated.tsx` player unchanged. This phase builds the way in, not a new player. The sheet states the real shape of the lesson it is about to play: for `sons.01.l1` that is 7 stages (warm, focus, input, practice, produce, check, cheat), 24 spoken passages and 7 mic moments across 3 stages. The mic never opens on its own — `narrated.tsx` deliberately waits for a tap, and the sheet must not promise otherwise.

### The language control

- **D-13:** `englishShown(band, pref)` stops being a boolean. Three states: **Français seul** (no translation in the lesson body), **Français et anglais** (the translation prints under each line, the current `true`), and **Anglais à la demande** (the translation exists, hidden until tapped). Board C6.
- **D-14:** The control takes the level-panel shape — a chip in the lesson header opening rows — for the same reason the panel won: adding a fourth mode costs one row instead of a redesign.
- **D-15:** It renders only on B1 and above. sons, A1 and A2 are taught in English, so the control never appears there. That absence is a state of the design, not an oversight.
- **D-16:** Answers are never glosses and never hide, in any of the three states: a flashcard back, the meaning a quiz asks for, a glossary tap. `english.logic.ts` already states this rule in its own header comment; the third state must not quietly break it.
- **D-17:** **Sequencing, and it is load-bearing — but only half of it is still open.** The two-state control **was committed on 2026-09-23 as `0b6a1a9`** ("feat(lessons): English toggle for B1 and above"), with typecheck clean, 5472/5472 node tests and 5/5 component tests green. Its own commit message ends "Not yet proven on device," and that is still true. So: **prove the two-state version on hardware before extending it to three states.** A device failure after the extension would be ambiguous between the shipped two-state work and the new third state. Hardware is available — 22.1-08's offline device pass ran on a Pixel 9 the same day.
  - An earlier note in `.planning/WORKSTREAMS.md` described this work as "uncommitted across thirteen files." That was true when written and is now false; `0b6a1a9` landed it. Ten gloss call sites were marked across the deck, section, story, scenario, listening, scene and word-card surfaces, and `lesson.tsx` provides `GlossContext` around the whole screen so the pager and the sheets read one answer.
  - The real open question for the third state is **migration**: `useStore.lessonEnglish` is a persisted boolean today, so every existing install has `true` or `false` stored. Three states need a value shape that reads those two safely rather than resetting the preference.

### Day one

- **D-18:** A first-run learner has no `Reprendre` to anchor on, so the trail shows a **start marker** on the first act instead. Board C4.
- **D-19:** Two dismissible cards sit above the first unit heading: a how-it-works card (a level is one scroll; italic headings are units; the rows under them are acts; the chip changes level) and a placement offer. Both dismissals persist across a relaunch. Neither may compete with the start marker for the primary action.
- **D-20:** The placement offer routes to the existing placement flow. This phase does not touch `placement.tsx`'s grading or its Première CTA.

### The paywall on the trail

- **D-21:** One component, two positions: at the **end of the A1 trail** (a boundary row naming what comes next), and **pinned above a locked band's trail** (a gate banner with the trail visible beneath it). Board C7.
- **D-22:** Locked acts stay readable and stay pressable in both positions. This is not new leniency — `den.tsx`'s own comment already states the intent: "locked units stay fully visible (what you would get), they just route to the paywall instead of the lesson." Visibility is not enforcement; the destination screen is the chokepoint.
- **D-23:** Both positions route to `/paywall?from=gate:levels`, which already maps to "Débloquez A2 et au-delà" / "Tous les niveaux après A1, débloqués. Sons et A1 restent gratuits pour toujours." Do not add a new `from` value and do not add a key to `PAYWALL_COPY`.
- **D-24:** `05-GATING-RULE.md` is unchanged by this phase. If planning finds a reason to change it, that is a finding to raise, not a change to make.

### Traps this phase will walk into

- **D-25:** **`Lesson.version` must not be bumped.** It keys learner checkmarks and XP. A2-era batch guards will demand a bump on any lesson edit; ignore them. There are 0 users today, but the constraint outlives the emptiness.
- **D-26:** **Do not create any file named `gloss*`.** `src/content/gloss.logic.ts` already exists and is the reading-passage glossary matcher that `ReadingPages` imports. It has been overwritten once by a Write that did not complain. The language control's module is `english.logic.ts`.
- **D-27:** **Two-lesson units exist: three of them.** `a1.30`, `a2.10` and `a2.35` each carry two lessons, and `den.tsx` opens `lessonIds[0]` only, so the second lesson of each is currently unreachable. Verified against the seed on 2026-09-23 (a prior note recorded only two of the three). The trail must decide what to do with them rather than inheriting the bug silently.
- **D-28:** **Deep links must keep working.** The scheme is `ealch`, not `wonerock`. Any route this phase deletes or renames breaks the adb deep-link recipe the project uses for device verification, which is also how this phase will be verified.
- **D-30:** **A temporary dev-only block currently deep-links into a route this phase deletes.** `app/den.tsx` carries an uncommitted `DEV-PREVIEW-B101` card, `__DEV__`-only, that pushes `/lessonoverview?key=b1.01.l1` so the unpublished b1.01 draft can be opened on a device. It belongs to the B1 content workstream, not to this phase, and it is the b1 authors' only way into that draft. Deleting the `lessonoverview` route (D-02) breaks it. Plan a replacement route into the trail for it rather than discovering it broken, and do not simply delete it — it is someone's working tool. Files involved: `app/den.tsx`, `app/lesson.tsx`, `src/components/LessonPager.tsx`, `src/content/schema.ts`, `src/services/content.ts`, `src/content/dev-preview-b101.json`.
- **D-29:** The trail has to be virtualized, and the jump-to affordance and position marker have to be good. **Measured against the shipped seed on 2026-09-23, because the canvas's "361 acts" is a plan figure, not a corpus figure:**

| Band | Units | Lessons | Lessons with acts | Acts |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| sons | 10 | 10 | **6** | 37 |
| a1 | 30 | 31 | 31 | 178 |
| a2 | 35 | 37 | 37 | 216 |
| b1 and up | **0** | 0 | 0 | 0 |

  The worst trail that exists today is **A2: 216 act rows plus 35 unit headings, about 251 rows**. That is testable on a device now, so the virtualization risk is real and measurable rather than hypothetical. B1's 361 acts are authored (`ff4fdf8`) but seed-absent, so four of the level panel's seven rows have no content behind them and cannot be exercised on-device from the seed.

- **D-31:** **RESOLVED 2026-09-23: author acts for the four act-less units before the trail ships.** `sons.01`, `sons.02`, `sons.03` and `sons.04` carry lessons with **zero** `acts` and 20 to 26 `sections` each; they predate the mission-journey shape. Rather than give the trail a second row vocabulary or a thin day-one screen, those four lessons get acts authored so the trail has one shape everywhere. Consequences planning must carry:
  - This is **content work, and it gates the phase**. It is not app code and it does not belong in the same plan as the trail. Sequence it as a prerequisite with its own verification, or the trail lands on a corpus it cannot render.
  - All four are narrated, so acte 00 already has something to offer on each of them while the acts are being written.
  - Board C4 draws six invented act rows for `sons.01`. Once the real acts exist, either the board is redrawn to match or the board is accepted as indicative. Say which.
  - `Lesson.version` **must not** be bumped by this authoring (D-25), and `ealch-admin/scripts/check-version-bumps.ts` will ask for one.
  - Watch the publish seam: a seed-direct edit can run ahead of Postgres. Run a publish dry run before calling this done.

- **D-32:** **RESOLVED 2026-09-23: promote c2 to a real level inside this phase.** `LEVELS` gains `'c2'`, along with the database enum and the tests, so the panel's seventh row is a real `Level` end to end rather than a display-only placeholder. This implements the 2026-09-22 decision that reversed the "c2 is a scoring band only" canon. Consequences planning must carry:
  - **This was chosen over the narrower option with the cost stated, and a later research pass raised the cost further.** `LEVELS` has **eight downstream consumers**, including `BANDS_RE` (which every content-id regex is built from) and six validator membership checks, plus the Postgres enum and the publish validators. The research pass independently recommended the display-only route for exactly this reason. The wider option stands as Paul's call; planning should scope it honestly rather than discover it.
  - It crosses into the **two migration systems** seam (one database, two migration paths). Treat the enum change as its own task with its own verification.
  - It closes a latent fail-open: `unitBand()` returns `null` for an unknown band and both gate chokepoints do `unitBand(...) ?? 'a1'`, so a `c2.*` unit would read as **free** today. Nothing leaks now because no c2 content exists. **Fix the fallback to fail closed regardless of the enum work** — that is a one-line safety change that should not wait on a schema migration.
  - `schema.ts:42-46` carries an authored rationale for excluding c2. Rewrite that comment; leaving it contradicting the code is how the next reader gets misled.
  - Separately and **not** to be conflated: do **not** widen `TRACKS`. It is `['sons','a1','a2']`, `Unit.track` is documented "absent past a2", and `T.trackLabels`/`trackDescs` are typed to three keys with real authored copy. `content.units(track)` therefore cannot source a B1+ trail at all; the trail needs a band-keyed accessor built on `unitBand(u.id)`, which the schema itself calls the reliable read.

- **D-33:** **`src/content/lesson-siblings.test.ts` fails as a whole file, not as one case.** It `readFileSync`s `app/lessonoverview.tsx` at module scope, so D-02 deleting that screen takes all 12 tests in the file down at import. It also asserts `den.tsx` still contains `params: { key: u.lessonIds[0] }`, which D-27 must change, and it records a previously *rejected* alternative (open the first incomplete lesson) that D-27 must not re-adopt by accident. Whatever replaces the sibling behaviour, the Fiche sheet has to carry the sibling rows or `a1.30.l2` — the A1 exam — becomes unreachable again.
- **D-34:** **The `PREMIÈRE` lock row is copy-pasted verbatim in six places**: `den.tsx`, `theme.tsx`, `flashthemes.tsx`, `dictationthemes.tsx`, `voicethemes.tsx`, `sentencethemes.tsx`. The trail's version (D-21/D-22) should extract it rather than make a seventh copy. Gate derivation is always `useFeature('levels.all')` plus `FREE_BANDS`, and must stay that way.
- **D-35:** **`MissionRich.tsx` reads `useShowEnglish()` directly**, bypassing `TX`'s `gloss` prop, so a third state delivered only through `gloss` would not reach it. And `Type.tsx`'s `gloss` branch is `if (gloss && !showEnglish) return null` — a pure `<Text>` with no press handling, so "anglais à la demande" (D-13) has no tap target today. The `EN:` tail case is worse: the translation is inline inside a string, so its target is a sentence fragment. This is real unresolved design work inside an otherwise approved decision, and planning must not assume the third state is a one-line change.
- **D-36:** **D-16 has no test.** `english.logic.test.ts` covers the pure functions only; nothing asserts that answer sites are un-`gloss`ed. The rule exists as a comment. Add the test with the third state, or the rule is one careless `gloss` away from breaking silently.
- **D-37:** **Acte 00 versus D-22.** `den.tsx` hides the `Narré` pill when the band is locked. That contradicts "locked acts stay readable and pressable": on a locked band today the narrated entry point disappears rather than routing to the paywall. Decide explicitly whether acte 00 shows on a locked band's trail.

- **D-38:** **Verify the sheet mechanism on a device before building on it.** The recommended route for D-02 is an expo-router screen with `presentation: 'formSheet'` — zero new dependencies, Android hardware back for free (the in-repo `BottomSheet.tsx` overlay has no `BackHandler`, which is a known bug class here), and it restores the `?key=<lessonId>` deep link D-28 needs. But expo-router's vendored `native-stack/types.d.ts` says formSheet "falls back to modal on Android", which contradicts the Kotlin actually shipped in react-native-screens 4.26.2. Two sources disagree. **Open a throwaway formSheet route on the device as the first check of the phase.** Five minutes, and it is the only load-bearing library claim in the research with a contradiction under it.
- **D-39:** **The one dependency worth adding is `@shopify/flash-list`**, and only if D-29's measurements demand it. Its v2 tarball ships no `android/`, no `ios/`, no podspec and no gradle file, so it is not autolinked and does not move the fingerprint runtime version — no new dev client, no EAS rebuild, OTA-deliverable. That matters because this project has had release builds die on native modules. Expo SDK 57 pins 2.0.2; install with `npx expo install`. `getItemType` is what makes heterogeneous rows work. `FlatList` + `getItemLayout` is not a real alternative here: row heights come from `typeMetrics()` and therefore vary with `PixelRatio.getFontScale()`, so there is no honest `getItemLayout` to write.
- **D-40:** **Ship the trail with non-sticky headings first.** `SectionList` defaults `stickySectionHeadersEnabled` to iOS-only, so sticky is a silent platform divergence; enabling it forces `scrollEventThrottle: 1`, a bridge event every frame, and carries an Android-only 15ms layout debounce. If a "which unit am I in" indicator is wanted, one pinned chip driven by `onViewableItemsChanged` gives the same information far more cheaply.
- **D-41:** **Scroll restoration: store the anchor row id, not a pixel offset.** The common path does not even need it — expo-router's native stack keeps the previous screen mounted under a push, and nothing sets `freezeOnBlur`, so trail to act to mission and back preserves scroll for free. The break cases are the five `router.replace('/den')` sites, and **two of them are acte 00's exit path in `narrated.tsx`**, so D-10 will hit this constantly. The explicit anti-pattern is in this repo: `speakmap.tsx` computes scroll from a hardcoded header and row height, which is wrong at font scale 1.3 and wrong the moment a dismissible card changes height. Also: `useProgress.resumeByMode` stores **full URLs**, so stale `/lessonoverview?key=...` entries need a migration drop or the first launch after the change is a mystery.
- **D-42:** **The three-state migration must map `true` to "both", not to "on demand".** Today's `true` prints the translation under every line. The boolean has exactly four consumers — the provider and pill in `lesson.tsx`, the whole rendering decision in `Type.tsx`, and one inline join in `MissionRich.tsx` — so the smallest change widens the **context value** and keeps `useShowEnglish(): boolean` as a derived helper, leaving all 13 `<TX gloss>` marker sites and `english.logic.test.ts` untouched. `useStore` is at `version: 6` with a live migrate ladder and `lessonEnglish` in `partialize`; replace the field rather than adding a second one that can disagree with it. Getting the mapping backwards is invisible in review and obvious on a device.
- **D-43:** **The flex-on-a-Text guard does not cover the screens this phase writes.** `sons-07-elision.test.ts` benchmarks five markup shapes on a Pixel 6 and finds that `flexShrink:1`, `flex:1`, a wrapping row and no flex at all **all clip** a title beside a glyph; only moving the glyph out of the row renders whole. But that test reads `ScenePlayer.tsx` only. Every Concept C trail row puts a title beside a glyph in a row, and the comment recording the correct shape lives at `lessonoverview.tsx:160` — **a file this phase deletes**. Carry the comment forward and extend the guard to the new components.
- **D-44:** **Font scale 1.3 is a non-monotonic bug, so test that exact value.** `type.logic.ts` records the measured Pixel 9 failure: display-role glyphs did not grow while the line box did, "À bientôt !" rendered as "À", eight of nine cards in one deck lost text — and it is **absent at 1.0, 1.15 and 1.8 and present at 1.3**. Sweeping a range and skipping 1.3 would miss it. Note also two conflicting 44px guards in the suite: one wants `hitSlop`, another wants `height: 44` and explicitly not `minHeight`. D-07's panel rows follow the first.
- **D-45:** **Two act-row data facts that will produce wrong-looking rows.** `deckTranche` is index-aligned with `acts` for 70 of 70 lessons that have it, so D-05's "mots partis en révision" number is safely derivable — but **107 of 423 tranches are empty and four lessons have acts with no tranche at all**, so about a quarter of act rows would read "0 mots" unless empty-state copy is authored. And **0 of 431 acts carry the `opener` field**, so D-03's act description needs a fallback rather than a blank.

## Deferred Ideas

- **Act and mission naming language.** D-08 settles the *unit heading*.
