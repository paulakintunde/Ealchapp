## Accomplishments

- `unitsInBand(corpus, band)` added beside `unitsInTrack` in `content.logic.ts`, keyed on `unitBand(u.id)` (the read schema.ts itself calls reliable) rather than `Unit.track` (absent past a2 by design). Exposed as `content.unitsOfBand(band)`; `content.units(track)` and `TRACKS` (still 3 members) both left untouched.
- `trail.logic.ts` created: the `TrailRow` union (orientation/placement/gate/heading/acte0/act/boundary) and `trailRows()`, composing a band's full trail from inputs alone — no store read, no entitlement decision beyond echoing `levelsAll`/`freeBands` (T-07.1-13). Verified against the shipped seed: the a2 band composes to exactly 35 headings + 216 acts = 251 rows, matching the measured_corpus table exactly.
- D-08/SC-3 (heading language) is driven by `ENGLISH_TAUGHT_BANDS` membership alone — proven not just by the two named literal cases (`sons.04` → "French Consonants", `a2.07` → "At the Restaurant", a synthesised b1 unit → its French `sub`) but by a test that MUTATES the constant itself at runtime and watches `headingLabel`'s output flip, closing off the possibility of a hidden hardcoded band list.
- D-27 (two-lesson units): `a1.30` composes to ONE heading row with both `a1.30.l1` and `a1.30.l2`'s acts under it, in `lessonIds` order — `a1.30.l2` (the A1 exam) is now a reachable act row. `lessonsOf`'s contract (resolve in the unit's own `lessonIds` order, matching `content.logic.ts`'s real `lessonsOfUnit`) is what makes this correct without `trailRows` re-sorting anything itself.
- D-11/D-37 (acte 00): `acteZeroLessonOf` lifted verbatim from `den.tsx:186-188`. 41 lessons qualify across the seed (sons 10, a1 31, a2 0) — but because `a1.30` carries TWO qualifying lessons under one heading and the predicate is per-unit, `trailRows` composes only 30 acte0 ROWS for a1 (resolved to `a1.30.l1`, the first). Both the lesson-level fact (SC-4's own contract, `acte-zero.test.ts`) and the row-level fact (`trail.logic.test.ts`) are pinned, with the discrepancy explained in both files so a future reader doesn't mistake one count for a typo of the other. D-37 is proven directly: a synthesised locked b1 band still emits an acte0 row, with `locked: true`, never zero rows.
- D-45 (release line): `releaseLine` never returns a fabricated "0 of something" — an empty or absent `deckTranche` slice gets the authored empty-state copy (`trailReleaseNone`), never an interpolated zero.
- D-21 (the two paywall touchpoints): a `gate` row lands at index 0 iff the whole band is locked; a `boundary` row lands after a1's last act iff the band is `a1` and `levelsAll` is false. Proven mutually exclusive by construction (different bands trigger each) and asserted so directly.
- D-18 (start marker): computed once across the WHOLE trail (not per-unit) — the first act row with `done: false`, only when `hasFreshResume` is false.
- D-19 (orientation/placement cards): appear before the first heading iff their dismissal flags are false; the `TrailRow` type itself gives neither a `startMarker` field, so they cannot compete with an act row for the primary action by construction, not by convention.
- `acte-zero.test.ts` additionally source-asserts `app/narrated.tsx` never auto-starts the recogniser (`stt.listen` is reachable only from `onPress={mic}`, never a mount-time `useEffect`) — CRLF-normalised, read inside the test body — so plan 09's pre-roll sheet cannot promise a mic the player does not honour, and resolves `sons.01.l1`'s real narration shape through `flattenNarration`/`stageRanges` (7 stages, 24 spoken segments, 7 mic moments across 3 stages) rather than a hand count, matching D-12's stated numbers exactly.
- Every i18n key phase 07.1 needs (trail cards/release/gate copy, the pre-roll sheet, `bandNames`/`bandDescs`/`bandPanelTitle`/`bandChipLabel`/`bandPremiereLine` for the seven-band level panel, and the three-state language control's title/labels/subs/reveal/chip labels) authored in `strings.ts`, in both `fr` and `en` tables, in this one plan. `trackLabels`/`trackDescs` (the dying three-track Den) and `PAYWALL_COPY` (D-23) both left untouched. `bandDescs` reuses `T.uSoon`'s own text for the four unauthored bands (b1-c2) rather than inventing a second empty-state string.
- A synthesised 30-unit/12-act-per-lesson b1 fixture composes to 390 rows (30 headings + 360 acts) — labelled explicitly as a projection, not a measurement, since the shipped seed carries zero B1 units.
