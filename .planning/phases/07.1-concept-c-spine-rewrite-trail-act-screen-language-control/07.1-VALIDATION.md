## Device Measurements

### D-38 — formSheet mechanism (Pixel 6, `21041FDF600BMN`, 2026-09-24, pre-settled before this plan's execution)

**Verdict: REAL BOTTOM SHEET, not a full-screen-modal fallback.** Dev client, Metro 8082,
throwaway `devsheet` route (`sheetAllowedDetents: [0.6, 1.0]`), since removed (`git stash`:
"07.1 D-38 probe (devsheet), run 2026-09-24, verdict recorded in 07.1-02-PLAN.md").

| Check | Result | Screenshot |
|---|---|---|
| Opens as sheet vs modal | Bottom sheet at the 0.6 detent, den visible and dimmed above it, rounded top corners. Not a modal. | `evidence/d38/01-sheet.png` |
| Drag down to dismiss | Yes — dismisses back to the den | `evidence/d38/02-after-drag.png` |
| Drag up to larger detent | Yes — snaps to the 1.0 detent | `evidence/d38/03-drag-up.png` |
| Hardware back (`KEYCODE_BACK`) | Closes the sheet, leaves the den in place. App does not exit. | `evidence/d38/04-after-back.png` |
| Font scale 1.3 | Still presents as a sheet | `evidence/d38/05-font13.png` |
| Device / font scales | Pixel 6, 1.0 and 1.3 | — |

Three defects recorded for plans 05, 06, 09 to design against (not fixed here, none is a
reason to fall back to `BottomSheet.tsx`):
1. The sheet paints only its content height — below the last child is unpainted app
   background. Needs `contentStyle: { backgroundColor: <sheet surface> }` or a
   fill-the-detent root.
2. A 1.0 detent runs under the status bar — cap the top detent below 1.0 (0.92 suggested)
   or pad with `useSafeAreaInsets().top` when expanded.
3. Font scale 1.3 drops text tails inside the sheet (same defect as
   `ealch-font-scale-drops-text`; the sheet does not exempt a surface from the house rule).
