**The consumer-side contract** is `src/components/LessonKeyIntro.tsx:18-27`. Read its header: *"dismissing (Skip or finishing) always marks it seen, but seeing it again never un-marks it."* D-19's two cards need exactly that.

Note plan 08 bumped `useProgress` persist to **v5** and dropped stale `/lessonoverview` and `/missions` routes, so a dev device's `resumeByMode.lesson` may legitimately be absent after that migration and will correctly show the start marker.
