## New decisions (locked 2026-10-01)

### French pronunciation under TalkBack

- **D-13:** Spike first, then choose. Plan 05 measures on the Pixel 6 whether any Android mechanism makes TalkBack read a French string in a French voice. Candidates: a `LocaleSpan` through a small native text patch or an Expo module; RN 0.86's Android handling of `accessibilityLanguage`; a `fr-FR` content description.
  - If a mechanism works, tag every authored-French text node through `TX lang="fr"`, the one primitive everything flows through.
  - If none works, the fallback is: every French word, line or sentence that the learner must hear has an adjacent, labelled play action using the app's French TTS (`T.playAudioA11y`), and that pairing becomes the audited rule.
  - Alternatives: (b) fallback only, no spike; (c) defer to the UDL backlog. Recommended because this is the app's core content.

### Feedback and announcements

- **D-14:** Every graded answer announces its result once, in the interface language, and includes the correct answer when the learner was wrong. Examples: "Correct", or "Not quite. The answer is: *la maison*". This covers quiz, mission, exercise, dictation, sentence and exam practice. Use `AccessibilityInfo.announceForAccessibility` at the grading call site, not a polite live region on a visual node that may re-render. Alternative: announce only right or wrong, with the answer reachable by swiping.
- **D-15:** Errors, toasts and changes such as "saved", "downloaded" or "offline" announce politely. Purely decorative motion (celebrations, mascot) announces nothing.

### Operability

- **D-16:** No action is reachable only by swipe or long press. Every swipe deck gets explicit Previous and Next actions, as `DeckNavigation` does. Every long-press behaviour (slow playback, the level panel on Den) also exists as a named `accessibilityActions` entry. The scope includes `LessonPager`, the `LessonRich` swipe sections, `QuizRoundsView`, `WordCardXL` and `den.tsx:278`.
- **D-17:** Icon-only and small controls have a minimum 48 dp touch target, through `hitSlop` where the visual size must stay.

### Large text on the learning path

- **D-18:** A11Y-04 extends from the flashcards route to every card-like surface on the learning path. That means in-lesson flashcards, voice prompt cards, `WordCardXL`, mission and quiz cards, and scene bubbles. The D-10 to D-12 pattern applies: content scrolls inside a bounded face, nothing truncates, and controls stay put. `numberOfLines` survives only on labels whose full text is available elsewhere, and each surviving use is listed in the audit.
- **D-19:** Test scales are 1.0, 1.3 and the device maximum (2.0 on Android 14 and later), plus the largest display size.
- **D-20:** Changing font scale or display size mid-task keeps the learner on the same screen with their state. The fix is to declare `fontScale|density` in `configChanges` through a config plugin, so RN re-lays out in place, rather than persisting navigation state.

### Theme

- **D-21:** In Automatic, the resolved mode always comes from the OS value and never from the app's own native override. The Settings label and the palette must never disagree with `cmd uimode night` after any sequence of taps, including rapid toggling.

### Evidence standard (applies to every stage)

- **D-22:** A device pass is recorded per control, not per group. Each row holds:
  - the screen;
  - the control;
  - the verbatim TalkBack utterance (transcribed by the tester, or captured from TalkBack's "Copy last utterance");
  - the activation outcome;
  - focus after activation;
  - the account type (free or premium);
  - the font scale;
  - the build id;
  - pass or fail.

  A summary line is never a pass. A stage gate cannot close with a Pending row. Locked-content checks use a free account; premium checks use a premium account.

### Claude's Discretion
