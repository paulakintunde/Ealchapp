<objective>
Make the paywall explain what the user just hit (D-08), and make the exam gates able to say so at all.

Purpose: `paywall.tsx` already destructures `from` (line 58) but uses it for exactly one thing — a `track('paywall_viewed', { from })` call — and then shows the same generic sell screen whether the user was blocked by an A2 lesson, the coach cap, the roleplay cap, or an exam paper. `exam.tsx` and `exam-paper.tsx` pass no param at all on their three `/paywall` pushes, so the exam case cannot even be distinguished.

UI-SPEC resolves 05-RESEARCH.md's Open Question 1: `gate:examiner` gets its own third render branch, an explainer with a dismiss-only CTA, because no exam-tier purchase surface exists in the app today.

Output: one copy map, one new render branch, three call-site params, one wiring test.

Also closes by verification (no code change): D-03 — 05-RESEARCH.md Pitfall 1 established that `featureRows` already has exactly three entries and no `audio.packs` bullet exists to remove. Task 2 asserts this rather than scheduling a removal that would find nothing.

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-05-16 | Spoofing | A deep link forging `?from=gate:coach` or any other value | accept | `from` selects display copy only. | T-05-18 | Information disclosure | Misleading purchase promise on the exam gate | mitigate | **This is the real risk this plan exists to close.** Because `'examiner'` is not in `PREMIERE_FEATURES`, routing an exam gate to the Première plan picker would take money for something that does not unlock what the user tried to open.
