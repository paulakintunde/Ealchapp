**Rules that are not negotiable:**

1. **Every finding names something.** A theme slug, a unit id, a lesson id, a
   paper id. ROADMAP Success Criterion 1 requires it. "Several A2 units" is not a
   finding; "a2.17, a2.21 and a2.24" is.
2. **Every Evidence block carries the raw query plus its result, or a `path:line`
   excerpt.** No finding may cite `corpus:probe`, `content:parity`, a CEFR or
   nasal heuristic, a spine-drift test, or a guard's exit code as its evidence
   (D-03). If such a tool prompted the investigation, say so in **Checked**, and
   still put the direct read in **Evidence**.
3. **Every finding has a Scope estimate OR a Deferral.** Never neither, never
   both. D-06 requires one of the two.
4. **No finding is fixed here.** This phase authors no content. A scope estimate
   describes future work; it does not start it.
5. **Prohibited language:** do not write "v1", "simplified", "static for now",
   "placeholder", "basic version", or "will be wired later" anywhere in a scope
   estimate. A scope estimate describes the full fix, sized — not a reduced one.
6. **Mark D-07 eligibility on every finding.** Plan 06 reads that flag to decide
   which findings get a ROADMAP stub phase. "Phase-worthy" means comparable in
   scope to a full lesson/unit build or a multi-paper audio re-render. A single
   thin lesson or a single unlistened paper is NOT phase-worthy — it stays in
   GAPS.md as a named, scoped item.
