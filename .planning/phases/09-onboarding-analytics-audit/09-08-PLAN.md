|---|---|---|---|---|
| T-09-17 | Information disclosure | Outbox at rest | medium | Entries hold only allowlisted properties (no email, answers, transcripts, raw referrer); the PostHog key is stripped and re-added at send. |
| T-09-18 | Denial of service | Outbox growth / resend loop | medium | 200-entry cap, 14-day expiry, 4xx never retried, flush stops at the first retryable failure and never blocks a learner action. |
| T-09-19 | Repudiation | Duplicate delivery | low | Resend keeps the original event ID, so a capture that was accepted but looked failed can be deduplicated by ID in PostHog. |
