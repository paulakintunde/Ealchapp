LA FRUSTRATION
You didn't fail French. French classes failed you.
You can't hear it.
Real French isn't the listening exam. It's liaisons, swallowed syllables, and « chais pas ». Ealch trains your ear on French as it's actually spoken — at full speed.
You freeze when it's your turn.
You translate in your head, and by the time the sentence is ready, the moment has passed. Camille keeps the conversation going at your pace — no sighing, no switching to English.
You write like a textbook.
Correct, polite, and completely unnatural. Learn to write the way French people actually write — from a text to a friend to an email to your landlord.
FONCTIONS
Built around the moment you speak.
SPEAK MODE
Conversations that talk back
Order at the café. Argue with the plumber. Sit a mock DELF oral. Camille plays the other side — in real French, at real speed, adjusted to your level.
LE RAPPORT
Feedback you can act on
After every session: what slipped — the missed liaison, the wrong gender, the tense that betrayed you — and the why behind each one. Not a score. A plan.
SMART REVIEW
Your mistakes, not a syllabus
The words you fumbled and the structures you dodged come back as drills — timed to the moment you'd otherwise forget them.
DICTÉE & L'OREILLE
Ear training on real speech
Dictation and listening drills built on how French sounds in the street, not in the studio. The gap between « je ne sais pas » and « chais pas » closes here.
LA MÉTHODE
Three steps, every day.
01
Parlez.
Pick a scenario and speak first — out loud, imperfectly, from day one. Camille meets you exactly at your level.
02
Lisez le Rapport.
See exactly what slipped and why — pronunciation, liaison, grammar — while the conversation is still fresh.
03
Révisez.
Today's mistakes become tomorrow's drills. Ten minutes a day, and the freeze slowly disappears.

Every learner says the same three things.
"I understand my teacher — but not actual French people."
Because teachers slow down. Camille doesn't have to — she adjusts to your ear, then speeds up as it sharpens.
"They hear my accent and switch to English."
The fastest way to lose your practice partner. Camille never switches. Every session is French, start to finish.
"I know the word. It just won't come out."
Passive knowledge isn't speaking. Ealch drills retrieval out loud — until the word arrives before the silence does.
