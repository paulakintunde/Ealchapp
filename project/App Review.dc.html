NOW
Closes the learning loop
Smart Review queue
One spaced-repetition queue fed by every mode: words missed in Role Play, sounds flagged by the AI coach, and failed quiz items all land in a single "Due today" deck. Flashcards exist but currently live in isolation — mistakes elsewhere vanish.
Tap-to-save dictionary
Tap any French word anywhere — feed, lessons, chat transcript — for a definition, gender, and audio, with one-tap save to a personal notebook that feeds the review queue.
Placement test
A 3-minute adaptive check at onboarding (or from the Den) that places returning learners past A1 instead of relying on self-reported experience.
Streaks & weekly goals
A daily goal ring on Home and Profile, streak count with one "freeze" per week, tied to the reminder alarms already in Settings. The single biggest retention lever the app lacks.
NEXT
Rounds out the skills
Dictation & writing
Every mode today is listening or speaking; nothing asks the learner to produce written French. A dictation drill (hear it, type it, with an accent-character row) covers spelling, agreement, and homophones like ces / ses / c'est.
Offline downloads manager
Première advertises "Offline" but there's no UI for it. A downloads screen — pick decks, tracks, and lessons; see storage used — makes the promise real for commuters.
Widget & word of the day
A lock-screen / home-screen widget with the day's phrase and streak state, plus a word-of-the-day notification — cheap surface area that re-opens the app daily.
LATER
Grows the ecosystem
Friends & leagues
Weekly leaderboard among friends, plus "challenge a friend" on any flashcard deck. Social accountability compounds the streak system.
CEFR milestone certificates
On completing a Den level, generate a shareable certificate backed by the Feedback Report's confidence data — proof of progress, and free marketing.
Live tutor sessions
Book a 15-minute human conversation when the AI coach's confidence score plateaus — a natural premium upsell above Première.
