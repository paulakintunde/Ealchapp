## Pack layout

- **The c1 pack exists already** (`PACK_ORDER` a2, b1, b2, c1, c2, exam): 0
  lessons, 913 items, 0.35 MiB, kept at v79. All 913 rows are published; they
  ride in the c1 pack because the Speak trail's world 6 (`speak.6.1` to
  `speak.6.5`, the only c1 rows in `content_units`) names them. The local test
  corpus (`seedPlusPacks()`) shows only 29 of them because it is a cut, so
  reusable c1 rows must be probed in Postgres. Nothing in the pack code changes.
- **Size:** B2's pack is 15.22 MiB for 34 lessons, about 0.45 MiB a lesson; C1 at
  24 lessons comes to about 11 MiB, far under the 80 MiB ceiling. The legacy full
  snapshot is retired from v83, so its 50 MiB limit no longer applies.
- **Core growth:** 24 skeleton units, a few KB each; the free core stays about
  10 MiB (9.79 MiB in the Phase 0 dry run).
- **Cross-band borrowing:** a C1 lesson that reuses a B2 row leaves that row in
  the b2 pack (a borrowed item goes DOWN to the lowest band that names it, never
  up; pinned by a Phase 0 test). A device always fetches b2 before c1, and the
  publish check validates core + every band in fetch order. `pack-replay.ts`
  replays phones on older versions onto each publish.
- **Not bundled in the binary**, like B2: C1 arrives by download (C1 is
  PREMIÈRE).
- **Gate previews:** a dev build never loads the packs layer, so the gate preview
  fixture must hold the whole band (memory ealch-dev-build-no-packs-layer).

## What is different at C1

- **French at C1, English still authored.** Texts are authentic-length and
  unsimplified (300 to 500 words for reading, 2 to 4 minutes for listening;
  exam-format practice uses the tasks' own lengths: two texts of about 300-360
  words, recordings of 90-120 s heard once), but every learner string still
  carries English behind the EN pill. Learner is tu.
- **Workshops weigh the task.** Writing tasks run 250 to 400 words; the rubric
  adds nuance and precision of vocabulary and argument structure to the B2
  criteria. One `task` per lesson, one word range: the full DALF pair (synthèse +
  essai, about 470 words) does not fit and needs Paul's call for c1.10.
- **Recognition stays recognition** (c1.02, c1.03), as b2.08 and b2.09 did.
- **Cite by trail position** (« la leçon 20 du B2 »), never a raw id.
- **Reuse published rows by id, Postgres first;** read any legacy c1 row before
  reusing it (straight apostrophes, « ? » spacing, one wrong mood in
  `fr.c1.francais-professionnel-avance.024`).
- **Tag** « C1 · LEÇON NN »; unit `c1.NN`, lesson `c1.NN.l1`.
