# b1.20 « La négation avancée » · chapter opener artwork

Eleven images, one per act, shown on the chapter card the pager draws before each
act's first mission (`LessonAct.opener.imageRef`).

- **Where the files go:** `ealch-v2/assets/lessons/negation-avancee/`
- **Filenames, exactly:** `act01.jpg` … `act11.jpg` (the lesson already points at
  `lessons/negation-avancee/actNN.jpg`, so a different name renders nothing).
- **Format:** landscape 4:3, JPEG.
- **Before anything renders:** each ref must be registered in
  `ealch-v2/src/content/lessonImages.ts`. `require()` resolves statically, so a
  ref registered before its file exists breaks the bundle. Generate the files
  first, then register them. This file does NOT touch lessonImages.ts.
- Every prompt below stands alone: paste one into ChatGPT (or the Fal image
  script) and it produces that act's picture without any other context. The
  house style block is repeated in full in each prompt on purpose.
- **No lettering of any kind** in any image, no close-up faces, and nothing that
  needs a caption to be understood: the opener already carries the act title and
  the promise line underneath. Where a picture holds a card, a screen or a sheet
  of paper, it is BLANK.
- **The single visual metaphor is THE EMPTY SPACE WHERE SOMETHING SHOULD BE.**
  In all eleven images something is missing and the picture shows the shape of
  the gap: an open key box with nothing inside, a paler patch on a wall where a
  frame used to hang, a shelf with a single jar left, a chair pulled back with
  nobody in it. That is the reframe: ne opens a gap, and the second word gives
  it its shape. Act 3 is the deliberate inversion (everything is missing
  EXCEPT one thing), and act 10 shows the one small piece that falls away while
  the gap stays readable. The conceptual images (acts 2, 3, 6, 8, 10) alternate
  with quiet human or street scenes (acts 1, 4, 5, 7, 9, 11) so eleven pictures
  do not read as eleven diagrams. The setting leans on Québec City in late
  autumn and winter, matching the lesson's rented flat.
