# b1.17 « L'ordre des pronoms » — chapter opener artwork

Eleven images, one per act, shown on the chapter card the pager draws before each
act's first mission (`LessonAct.opener.imageRef`).

- **Where the files go:** `ealch-v2/assets/lessons/ordre-pronoms/`
- **Filenames, exactly:** `act01.jpg` … `act11.jpg` (the lesson already points at
  `lessons/ordre-pronoms/actNN.jpg`, so a different name renders nothing).
- **Format:** landscape 4:3, JPEG.
- **Before anything renders:** each ref must be registered in
  `ealch-v2/src/content/lessonImages.ts`. `require()` resolves statically, so a
  ref registered before its file exists breaks the bundle. Generate the files
  first, then register them. This file does NOT touch lessonImages.ts.
- Every prompt below stands alone: paste one into ChatGPT (or the Fal image
  script) and it produces that act's picture without any other context. The
  house style block is repeated in full in each prompt on purpose.
- **No lettering of any kind** in any image, no close-up faces, and nothing that
  needs a caption to be understood: the opener already carries the act title and
  the promise line underneath. **This includes numerals.** The lesson's queue has
  five numbered places and none of the numbers may be drawn: order is shown by
  left-to-right position, by size and by colour, never by a digit.
- **The single visual metaphor is THE QUEUE AND ITS ANCHOR.** A row of small
  coloured tokens, always in the same left-to-right order, pressed flat against
  one larger dark form that stands for the verb. The tokens never change their
  order and never leave the large form. Act 6 is the one image where the whole
  row is on the OTHER side of the large form, and act 7 puts it back. The five
  tokens recur from act 2 onward, always the same five shapes in the same five
  colours, so a learner who saw act 2 recognises them in act 11 without being
  told. Acts 1, 4, 6, 8 and 11 are quiet human scenes; acts 2, 3, 5, 7, 9 and 10
  are conceptual shapes, so eleven images do not read as eleven diagrams.
