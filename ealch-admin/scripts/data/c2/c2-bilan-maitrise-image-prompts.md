# c2.14 « Bilan de maîtrise » : chapter opener artwork

Eight images, one per act, shown on the chapter card the pager draws before each
act's first mission (`LessonAct.opener.imageRef`).

- **Where the files go:** `ealch-v2/assets/lessons/c2-14-bilan-maitrise/`
- **Filenames, exactly:** `act01.jpg` … `act08.jpg` (the lesson already points at
  `lessons/c2-14-bilan-maitrise/actNN.jpg`, so a different name renders nothing).
- **Format:** landscape 4:3, JPEG.
- **Before anything renders:** each ref must be registered in
  `ealch-v2/src/content/lessonImages.ts`, with alt text. `require()` resolves
  statically, so a ref registered before its file exists breaks the bundle. This
  file does NOT touch `lessonImages.ts` and no image was generated by the author
  of this draft; Paul draws the images himself from the prompts below, drops
  them in the folder, then registers them, once, by script.
- Every prompt below stands alone: paste one into an image generator and it
  produces that act's picture without any other context. The house style block
  is repeated in full in each one, on purpose.
- **No lettering of any kind** in any image, no close-up faces, nothing that
  needs a caption to be understood: the opener already carries the act title
  and the promise line underneath.
- **The single visual metaphor is THE ORCHESTRA** (the lesson's own reframe:
  "thirteen instruments tuned separately; the capstone is the piece where
  they finally play together"). A small, warm-toned chamber ensemble of simple
  rounded instrument shapes (a stringed instrument, a wind instrument, a small
  drum, a tall standing instrument) sits at the centre of each image. Early
  acts (2 to 6) show ONE OR TWO instruments at a time, each resting alone or
  being tuned by a simple rounded figure, never yet playing together. Act 7
  shows one instrument's bow or mallet paused mid-stroke, silent, among
  instruments that are already playing: the one skill not yet joined in. Act 8
  is the only image where every instrument plays at once, bows and mallets all
  in motion together. Act 1 shows the instruments still in their cases, unopened,
  beside a single open ledger: the portfolio, before any of them has played.
  No musical score, no visible notes, no text of any kind.
