# c2.11 « Compréhension orale experte » : chapter opener artwork

Nine images, one per act, shown on the chapter card the pager draws before each
act's first mission (`LessonAct.opener.imageRef`).

- **Where the files go:** `ealch-v2/assets/lessons/c2-11-ecoute-experte/`
- **Filenames, exactly:** `act01.jpg` … `act09.jpg` (the lesson already points at
  `lessons/c2-11-ecoute-experte/actNN.jpg`, so a different name renders nothing).
- **Format:** landscape 4:3, JPEG.
- **Before anything renders:** each ref must be registered in
  `ealch-v2/src/content/lessonImages.ts`, with alt text. `require()` resolves
  statically, so a ref registered before its file exists breaks the bundle. This
  file does NOT touch `lessonImages.ts` and no image was generated by the author
  of this draft; Paul draws the images himself from the prompts below, drops
  them in the folder, then registers them, once, by script.
- Every prompt below stands alone: paste one into an image generator and it
  produces that act's picture without any other context. The house style block
  is repeated in full in each one, on purpose.
- **No lettering of any kind** in any image, no close-up faces, nothing that
  needs a caption to be understood: the opener already carries the act title
  and the promise line underneath.
- **The single visual metaphor is THE SIGNAL** (the lesson's reframe: "look for
  the signal, even in the noise"). A small, steady, warm glowing point of light
  (a lantern or soft orb) represents the signal throughout every act: the
  frame and the dividing line the learner already knows how to find. Visual
  "bruit" gathers AROUND this light without ever extinguishing it: crossing
  translucent ripples for overlapping voices (act 2), a shifting halo colour
  for a register change (act 3), a distinct abstract textile or pattern motif
  framing the light for each region (acts 4 to 6, never a flag, never a face,
  never a caricature), fine static grain clouding the air for degraded audio
  (act 7), all of the above stacked at once around the same light (act 8), and
  a calm, orderly version of the light alone on a clean desk for the exam
  (act 9). The light itself never changes size or colour at its core in any
  image: only what surrounds it changes.

## The house STYLE block

Verbatim from `ealch-admin/scripts/gen-rythme-images.mjs`, repeated at the end of every prompt below:

> Flat editorial vector illustration, in the style of a modern French language textbook. Muted warm palette: cream and bone backgrounds, dusty slate blue, terracotta, warm ochre, sage green. Soft rounded geometric figures with simple calm faces, no black outlines, gentle flat shading. Subtle paper grain texture over the whole image. Warm even light. No text unless explicitly described. Uncluttered composition with generous empty space. Absolutely no lettering, captions, watermarks or logos beyond what is described.
