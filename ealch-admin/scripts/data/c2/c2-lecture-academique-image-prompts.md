# c2.12 « Lire des textes académiques denses » : chapter opener artwork

Eight images, one per act, shown on the chapter card the pager draws before
each act's first mission (`LessonAct.opener.imageRef`).

- **Where the files go:** `ealch-v2/assets/lessons/c2-12-lecture-academique/`
- **Filenames, exactly:** `act01.jpg` … `act08.jpg` (the lesson already points at
  `lessons/c2-12-lecture-academique/actNN.jpg`, so a different name renders nothing).
- **Format:** landscape 4:3, JPEG.
- **Before anything renders:** each ref must be registered in
  `ealch-v2/src/content/lessonImages.ts`, with alt text. `require()` resolves
  statically, so a ref registered before its file exists breaks the bundle. Paul
  draws the images himself in ChatGPT from the prompts below, drops them in the
  folder, then registers them, once, by script (B1-BUILD-PLAYBOOK.md §6.19). This
  file does NOT touch `lessonImages.ts` and no image was generated by the author
  of this draft.
- Every prompt below stands alone: paste one into ChatGPT and it produces that
  act's picture without any other context. The house style block is repeated in
  full in each one, on purpose.
- **No lettering of any kind** in any image, no close-up faces, nothing that needs
  a caption to be understood: the opener already carries the act title and the
  promise line underneath.
- **The single visual metaphor is DÉPLIER (unfolding).** A dense sheet of paper,
  folded accordion-style into tight panels, like a folded letter or map, which
  only reveals what it is carrying once it is opened out, panel by panel. Four
  thread/panel colours from the house palette recur, in the same role, across
  all eight images, so a reader who sees several openers in a row can tell the
  four moves apart without a caption: dusty slate blue (l'armature, the
  sentence's own backbone), terracotta (la réserve empilée, the stacked hedge),
  warm ochre (l'attribution de la citation), sage green (la chaîne nominale).
  Act 7 adds a small clock or hourglass motif (the time constraint); act 1 and
  act 8 are the only two with a human figure in the room, bookending the set.

## The house STYLE block

Verbatim from `ealch-admin/scripts/gen-rythme-images.mjs`, repeated at the end of every prompt below:

> Flat editorial vector illustration, in the style of a modern French language textbook. Muted warm palette: cream and bone backgrounds, dusty slate blue, terracotta, warm ochre, sage green. Soft rounded geometric figures with simple calm faces, no black outlines, gentle flat shading. Subtle paper grain texture over the whole image. Warm even light. No text unless explicitly described. Uncluttered composition with generous empty space. Absolutely no lettering, captions, watermarks or logos beyond what is described.
