# TOPICS — DALF C1

**Blueprint:** `dalf-c1-2026.10` · **64 situations, all C1**
**Compiled:** 2026-10-06

Our own topic bank. General social themes, built from the CEFR C1 descriptors
and the themes that carry published items in `content_themes`. Nothing here is
drawn from an exam paper. See [README.md](README.md).

---

## How to use this

Organised by **suit**, as the DELF bank is: what differs between slots is shape,
not difficulty. A topic that sustains a seven-minute interview is useless as a
ninety-second flash, and a dossier topic must split into documents that cross.

A paper spends **7 or 8 topics**:

| Suit | Slot | Per paper | In bank |
|---|---|---|---|
| `CO-L` | Listening part 1: one long document, two plays | 1 | 9 |
| `CO-S` | Listening part 2: short radio extracts, one play | 2 or 3 | 22 |
| `CE` | Reading: one text of ideas, 1500-2000 words | 1 | 9 |
| `PE` | Writing: synthèse dossier AND the essai built on it | 1 | 9 |
| `PO` | Speaking: two subjects drawn, one chosen | 2 | 15 |

Five papers spend at most 40 of 64 (5 / 15 / 5 / 5 / 10 against 9 / 22 / 9 / 9 /
15), which leaves room to reject a topic that will not carry and for a sixth
paper in every suit.

Rules:

1. **No topic is reused across DALF papers.** Each paper exports `TOPICS` as
   data; `topicLedgerViolations` in scripts/dalf/paper-rules.ts reads them all.
2. **`CO-L`, `CE`, `PE` and `PO` topics need tension.** At C1 the dear questions
   test stance and the dossiers need angles that cross. Where the tension is not
   obvious from the row, the row names it.
3. **A `PE` row is one theme for two tasks.** The synthèse reports the dossier;
   the essai argues a different question on the same theme, from the role and to
   the addressee the row gives. The genre rotates across papers.
4. **A `PO` row is a question with no single right answer.** The exposé needs a
   plan and the entretien needs two attackable sides.
5. **`CO-S` rows carry exactly two or three answerable points**, because the
   extract is heard once and answered in 50 seconds.
6. **Every proper noun in a finished stimulus is invented** (STANDARD-common §6,
   STANDARD-dalf-c1 §2).
7. **Two subjects drawn together must differ in theme**, so the choice is real.

### Excluded: the FEI demo paper's topics

Never authored, in any suit, nor approached sideways: measuring happiness or
national wealth; a « living fossil » fish or any rediscovered species; postcards;
career change and guest houses; linguistic diversity, endangered languages or
which languages schools teach; whether work is needed for happiness, the
virtues of idleness, automation shrinking work; biodiversity and the human role
in preserving it.

## Coverage against the corpus

Measured in Postgres (`content_items`, read only) on 2026-10-06, not asserted.

**Subject themes resolve at B2, not C1.** Every `Theme` above carries at least 80
published B2 items. Almost none carries C1 items: published C1 content sits in
skill and register themes, not subject themes. Only `philosophie` (10),
`ethique` (6), `methode-scientifique` (6) and `decouvertes` (5) have any C1
subject items.

So a closed DALF task routes misses (`targetItemIds`) to two pools:

| Pool | Themes (published C1 items) | For |
|---|---|---|
| C1 skill | `strategies-de-comprehension` (356), `connecteurs-logiques` (132), `nuances-et-registres` (319), `rhetorique` (338), `langue-soignee` (182) | the comprehension skill the question tests: inference, stance, connectors, register |
| B2 subject | the row's `Theme` | the vocabulary of the document's subject |

`discours-dexamen` (574 C1) is the natural home for synthèse, essai and exposé
practice, but open tasks carry no `targetItemIds`.

Theme load: `economie`, `examens-et-diplomes`, `traditions` and `rp-loisirs` at
four rows each; `communaute`, `musees`, `tourisme`, `gouvernement`,
`rp-technologie` at three. The ledger forbids reuse, so a
theme appearing four times cannot appear twice in one paper unless the planner
is broken; still, rule 7 asks for two different themes in one paper's PO pair.

Themes with B2 items still unused here, for a sixth paper: `philosophie`,
`disciplines`, `emotions`, `bien-etre` (but nothing on happiness),
`collegues`, `quebec-et-francophonie` (but nothing on language diversity),
`decouvertes`, `rp-sante`, `opinions-et-avis`.
