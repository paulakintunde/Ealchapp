# Pilot narration script — A1 · Au café (ordering a coffee)

Written with the §4 authoring brief in `AUDIO-LESSON-SCRIPT-SYSTEM.md`.
Level **a1** · theme **cafe** · ratio ~70% EN / 30% FR · pause cue `[pause:4]` · voice: Camille.
Markup per §3: plain text = English voice, `[FR]…[/FR]` = French voice, `[pause:N]` = interactive
pause (countdown + Continue), `[sfx:ding]` = "your turn" cue, `[dir:…]` = advisory delivery note.

Corpus items woven in (existing `fr.a1.cafe` theme): `un café`, `s'il vous plaît`, `je voudrais`,
`l'addition`, `merci`, `bonjour`. These are the item IDs the drills already key against, so the Den
recycles the same pool.

---

### 1. WARM / RECALL — Hook + welcome + objective *(this plays when the learner lands)*

[dir: warm, slow] [FR]Bonjour ![/FR] and welcome. Today we are going somewhere you'll go all the time in
France, a café. In the next few minutes you'll learn to walk up, order a coffee, and pay, all by
yourself, in French. It's short, it's useful, and you'll use it on day one of any trip. Let's warm up
your voice. Say hello with me. [sfx:ding] [FR]Bonjour ![/FR] [pause:4] Nice. That word, [FR]bonjour[/FR],
opens every café door in France. [FR]C'est parti ![/FR] Let's go.

### 2. FOCUS / VOCAB — concept breakdown

First, the drink. A coffee is [FR]un café[/FR]. Listen, then say it back. [FR]un café[/FR] [pause:4]
Now the magic word for being polite, please. In a café you say [FR]s'il vous plaît[/FR]. It sounds like
three little pieces. [FR]s'il vous plaît[/FR] [pause:4] Put them together and you can already order.
A coffee, please. [FR]Un café, s'il vous plaît[/FR]. Your turn. [sfx:ding] [FR]Un café, s'il vous
plaît[/FR] [pause:4] Beautiful. There's an even softer way to ask, I would like. That's
[FR]je voudrais[/FR]. Say it slowly. [FR]je voudrais[/FR] [pause:4] So, I would like a coffee, please,
becomes [FR]Je voudrais un café, s'il vous plaît[/FR]. Try the whole thing. [pause:4] Last one for now,
the bill. When you're ready to pay, you ask for [FR]l'addition[/FR]. [FR]l'addition[/FR] [pause:4]

### 3. INPUT / STORY — natural demo

Now listen to Léa order. Just listen, you don't have to speak yet. [FR]Bonjour ![/FR] [FR]Je voudrais
un café, s'il vous plaît.[/FR] [FR]Merci ![/FR] Then, a moment later. [FR]L'addition, s'il vous
plaît.[/FR] So, what happened there? Léa said hello, she asked for a coffee with please, she said thank
you, and at the end she asked for the bill. Four little steps, and every one of them you already know.

### 4. PRACTICE — guided drill

Let's swap one word. You know [FR]un café[/FR]. A tea is [FR]un thé[/FR]. So order a tea, please.
[sfx:ding] [pause:4] You would say [FR]Un thé, s'il vous plaît[/FR]. Again with, I would like.
[FR]Je voudrais un thé, s'il vous plaît[/FR]. Your turn. [pause:4] Now back to coffee, the polite way,
from the top. [pause:4] [FR]Je voudrais un café, s'il vous plaît[/FR]. Well done.

### 5. PRODUCE — learner output

Now it's all you. Imagine you just walked in. Greet the server, then order whatever you like, a coffee
or a tea, and don't forget please. Say it out loud now. [sfx:ding] [pause:4] However it came out, you
ordered in French. That's the whole skill.

### 6. CHECK — quick comprehension

Two quick checks. How do you ask for the bill? Say it. [pause:4] It's [FR]l'addition, s'il vous
plaît[/FR]. And how do you say, I would like? [pause:4] That's [FR]je voudrais[/FR]. If those came
back to you, you're ready.

### 7. CHEAT SHEET — recap + active outro

Here's everything from today. Hello, [FR]bonjour[/FR]. A coffee, [FR]un café[/FR]. Please, [FR]s'il
vous plaît[/FR]. I would like, [FR]je voudrais[/FR]. Thank you, [FR]merci[/FR]. The bill,
[FR]l'addition[/FR]. Six phrases, one real café order. Now here's your task, and I want you to answer
out loud. What will you order on your first day in Paris? Tell me now, in French. [sfx:ding] [pause:4]
[FR]Très bien ![/FR] You did it. I'm proud of you, and I'll see you in the next lesson. [FR]À
bientôt ![/FR]
